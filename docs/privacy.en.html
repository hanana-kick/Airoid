Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025

Airoid is a local Android app that turns your device into an AirPlay receiver. It does not collect personal information.

What the app does not do

There is no sign-up or login. The app does not request or store email addresses, advertising IDs, contacts, or location data.

Information that stays on your devices

  • The pairing name shown during an AirPlay connection is created on the device and is not sent elsewhere.
  • Screen and audio data move only between devices on the same network. They are not sent to a developer or third-party server.
  • AirPlay discovery needs access to the local network. It does not need an internet connection.

Permissions

  • A foreground service and a notification keep mirroring active and show the Quick Settings tile.
  • The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or storage access.

Storage and deletion

The app stores no personal information, so there is no personal data to retain or delete. When you uninstall the app, local data such as cache and settings is removed too.

Sharing

The app does not share personal information with or entrust it to another company or person.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from children under 14.

Security

Screen and audio data stay on the local network. The app is distributed as a signed APK.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and mention the change in the release notes.

Contact

For privacy questions, leave a message on GitHub Issues.
